ABOUT DAVIDO

David Adedeji Adeleke, known worldwide as Davido, born on November 21, 1992, is a multi-award-winning Afrobeats superstar, songwriter, record producer and entrepreneur. He owns a Record label known as Davido Music Worldwide(DMW).

A proud son of Africa, Davido's sound is a fusion of Afrobeats, R&B, Pop and his rich cultural roots. His mission? To put African music on the biggest stages in the world.

Ask your Questions

If you have more questions just follow Davido across social media platforms.

What does OBO mean?

OBO means "Omo Baba Olowo", a yoruba phrase which translates to "Son of a rich man". His other nicknames include: 001, Baddest, 30BG General(from his fanbase 30 billion gang).

Where is Davido from?

He was born in Atlanta, Georgia, USA but he's originally from Osun State specifically the town of Ede.

What is Davido's biggest song?

There are two common answers - FALL and UNAVAILABLE. Fall is historically his most iconic hit globally whereas Unavailable is his most streamed song with over 230 million streams on spotify.

What are his achievements?
  • Multiple "The Headies Award"
  • BET Awards wins for Best International Act
  • MTV Europe Music Awards wins for Best African Act
  • Numerous AFRIMA, Soundcity MVP, and Nigeria Entertainment Awards
How can i join the 30BG movement?
  • You can become part of the 30BG movement by following Davido on his official social media accounts.
  • Listening to and supporting his music on streaming platforms.
  • Engaging with other fans online using the #30BG hashtag.
  • Joining fan groups on platforms like X(Twitter), Facebook, Whatsapp, Telegram or Discord that are dedicated to 30BG
